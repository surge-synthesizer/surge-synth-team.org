Modules are the heart and soul of SpectrumWorx! Fifty-seven of them provide you with countless
ways of creating effects. As we already said, each module is a sophisticated audio processor in
and of itself, with its own, unique selection of adjustable parameters. The following pages
describe each module in some detail.

Modules are grouped into nine categories in the load menu:

| Category | Count | What's in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Pitch](/spectrumworx/manual/modules-pitch/) | 7 | Shifting, following, tuning and oscillating pitch |
| [Timbre](/spectrumworx/manual/modules-timbre/) | 8 | Filtering and reshaping the spectrum |
| [Time](/spectrumworx/manual/modules-time/) | 6 | Freezing, slicing, reversing and decaying |
| [Space](/spectrumworx/manual/modules-space/) | 3 | Frequency domain echo and reverb |
| [Phase](/spectrumworx/manual/modules-phase/) | 4 | Flattening, randomizing and flipping phase |
| [Loudness](/spectrumworx/manual/modules-loudness/) | 5 | Gain, dynamics and noise |
| [Combine](/spectrumworx/manual/modules-combine/) | 10 | Everything that mixes main with side channel |
| [Phase Vocoder](/spectrumworx/manual/modules-phase-vocoder/) | 9 | True-frequency versions of the pitch and eximploder modules |
| [Miscellaneous](/spectrumworx/manual/modules-misc/) | 5 | The ones that fit nowhere else |

Remember that every module also has its own Gain, Wet and Frequency range controls in the
[main window](/spectrumworx/manual/main-window/), and that almost every module parameter can be
driven by [its own LFO](/spectrumworx/manual/lfo/).
