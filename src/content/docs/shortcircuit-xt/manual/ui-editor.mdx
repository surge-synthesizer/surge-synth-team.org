## The header

The header runs across the top of every screen. It holds:

- **EDIT**, **MIX** and **PLAY** to switch screens.
- **Undo** and **Redo**
- The save button and the **Multi and Instrument IO** menu. See
  [Saving and loading](#saving-and-loading).
- **Tune** opens the [tuning](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/microtuning/) menu.
- **OMNI**, **MPE** or **Ch/Oct** sets the [MIDI channel mode](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/parts-multitimbral/).
- **!** stops all sound. Right-click for **All Sounds Off** and **All Notes Off**.
- **CPU** and **RAM** readouts, and the main output meter.
- The Shortcircuit logo opens the main menu, with zoom, themes, the log and the About screen.

## Layout

The **EDIT** screen has the parts, groups and zones sidebar on the left and the
[browser](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/ui-browser/) on the right. The centre shows whatever is selected.

The sidebar's **PARTS**, **GROUPS** and **ZONES** tabs choose what the centre edits.

- **ZONES** and **GROUPS** show the mapping pane at the top, four processors, the modulation
  matrix and routing, two envelopes, and the LFOs. In **GROUPS** these panes edit the group, and
  the sidebar adds the group's trigger conditions and settings.
- **PARTS** shows the part's macros and its four [FX](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/parts-fx/).

Cmd/Ctrl+1, 2 and 3 select zones, groups and parts. Alt+G swaps between groups and zones.

## Selection

Group and zone selections are separate, and both are kept when you switch tabs. The panes always
show the **lead** selection; an edit applies to everything selected.

In the **ZONES** tab:

- Click a zone to select it.
- Shift-click to select a run of zones; Cmd/Ctrl-click to add or remove one.
- Click a group to select all its zones.

In the **GROUPS** tab, click a group to select it; Shift or Cmd/Ctrl-click adds to the selection.

## The group and zone tree

- The **Part** menu at the top of the tree chooses which part to edit.
- **+** at the bottom adds a group. Drop zones on it to move them to a new group.
- Drag rows to reorder zones and groups, or to move zones between groups.
- **M** on a group row mutes the group.
- Right-click a group or zone for rename, copy, paste, duplicate, delete and move.

## Saving and loading

A whole instrument is a **multi** (`.scm`); a single part is a `.scp` file. The save menu offers
each in three forms:

- **Only**: refers to samples where they are on disk.
- **with Collected Samples**: copies the samples to a folder beside the file.
- **as Monolith**: stores the samples inside the file.

The same menu can export a part as SFZ, which keeps only a small subset of features.

Loads can be undone.

**Reset Engine To** starts again from an empty part, 16 empty parts, a basic synth, or your
templates in `Patches/Templates`. It does not ask for confirmation, and clears the undo history.
