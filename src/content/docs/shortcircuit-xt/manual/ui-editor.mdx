## The header

The header runs across the top of every screen. It holds:

- **EDIT**, **MIX** and **PLAY** to switch screens.
- **Undo** and **Redo**
- The name of the multi, or of the selected part while the sidebar is on **GROUPS** or
  **ZONES**. Click it to rename. The arrows beside it load the previous or next file in the folder
  it came from. Right-click it for the save and load menu.
- The disk button opens the **Save and Load** menu. See [Saving and loading](#saving-and-loading).
- **Tune** opens the [tuning](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/microtuning/) menu.
- **OMNI**, **MPE** or **Ch/Oct** sets the [MIDI channel mode](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/parts-multitimbral/).
- **!** stops all sound. Right-click for **All Sounds Off** and **All Notes Off**.
- **CPU** and **RAM** readouts, and the main output meter.
- The Shortcircuit logo opens the main menu, with tuning, zoom, themes, keyboard shortcuts, the
  log and the About screen. Right-clicking the header opens it too.

## Layout

The **EDIT** screen has the parts, groups and zones sidebar on the left and the
[browser](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/ui-browser/) on the right. The centre shows whatever is selected.

The sidebar's **PARTS**, **GROUPS** and **ZONES** tabs choose what the centre edits.

- **ZONES** and **GROUPS** show the mapping pane at the top, four processors, the modulation
  matrix and routing, two envelopes, and the LFOs. In **GROUPS** these panes edit the group, and
  the sidebar adds the group's trigger conditions and settings.
- **PARTS** shows the part's **MACROS** and **SETTINGS** tabs and its four
  [FX](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/parts-fx/).

Cmd/Ctrl+1, 2 and 3 select zones, groups and parts. Alt/Opt+G swaps between groups and zones.
Every shortcut can be changed; see
[Keyboard Shortcuts](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/keyboard-shortcuts/).

Loading a multi or part leaves the screens and tabs as they are; only a DAW session brings back
the view it was saved with. The mixer keeps its selected bus when the editor is reopened.

## Selection

Group and zone selections are separate, and both are kept when you switch tabs. The panes always
show the **lead** selection; an edit applies to everything selected.

In the **ZONES** tab:

- Click a zone to select it and make it the lead.
- Shift-click to select the run of zones from the last one clicked. Shift-Cmd/Ctrl-click adds
  the run to the selection.
- Cmd/Ctrl-click to add or remove one zone. Alt/Opt-click adds a zone and makes it the lead.
- Click a group to select all its zones. Cmd/Ctrl or Alt/Opt-click adds them to the selection.

In the **GROUPS** tab, click a group to select it. Shift, Shift-Cmd/Ctrl and Cmd/Ctrl-click work
as they do on zones.

A group can link its zones, so that selecting any of them selects them all, with the first as the
lead. Turn it on with **Link Zone Selection** in the group's menu, or **Link Zone Selection in
Selected Groups** in the mapping pane menu. Linked groups show a link glyph in the tree, and their
zones are hatched on the map.

With **Allow Zone Edits in Group Mode** on in the mapping pane menu, clicking and editing zones on
the map leaves the sidebar on **GROUPS**.

## The group and zone tree

- The **Part** menu at the top of the tree chooses which part to edit.
- **+** at the bottom adds a group. Drop zones on it to move them to a new group.
- The arrow on a group row folds and unfolds it.
- Double-click a row to rename it.
- **M** and **S** on a group row mute and solo it. They act on every selected group when the
  group is selected. Hold Alt/Opt to mute or solo that group alone, Shift to sweep to the nearest
  group already set, or Cmd/Ctrl to change that group only.

Drag rows to reorder zones and groups, or to move zones between groups. A line shows where they
will land. Dragging a selected row moves the whole selection; hold Shift when the drag starts to
move just that row. Hold Cmd/Ctrl or Alt/Opt at the drop to copy instead of move.

Glyphs on a row show its state:

- A speaker beside a zone while it plays a voice, and on a group while the group is sounding,
  including while its effects ring out.
- A keyboard on a group with a keyswitch, lit while it is the live articulation.
- A link on a group whose zones select together.
- A zone whose sample is missing is drawn in the warning colour.

## Zone and group menus

Right-click a zone or group for its menu. Right-clicking inside a selection of several acts on all
of them, and the menu is headed, for example, **3 Selected Zones**. Right-clicking anything else
acts on just that row. The **GROUPS** and **ZONES** panel menus hold the same items for the
selection, plus **Expand All Groups**, **Collapse All Groups**, **Auto-Expand Selected Groups**
and **Auto-Expand Selected Zones**. Right-clicking the map gives the zone menu as well.

The zone menu has:

- **Rename**, **Cut**, **Copy**, **Duplicate**, **Paste** and **Delete**. Paste lands after the
  clicked or lead zone.
- **Delete All Zones with Missing Samples**
- **Exchange Sample...** and **Reveal Sample in Finder** (Explorer on Windows, File Manager on
  Linux), for a single zone.
- **Select All Zones** and **Deselect All Zones**
- **Move to New Group**: to one empty group or a clone of the selected group, or each zone to its
  own.
- **Move to Existing Group**
- **Batch Functions**: move root keys to the first, centre or last key of each zone, and apply or
  remove key and velocity crossfades between zones.

The group menu has:

- **Rename**, **Copy**, **Cut**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**
- **Paste with Zones** and **Paste without Zones**, after the clicked or lead group, and **Paste
  Zone** into the clicked group.
- **Delete Empty Groups**, **Add New Group** and **Create Empty Zone**
- **Link Zone Selection**
- **Select All Groups** and **Deselect All Groups**

The menus on rows and on the map end with the **Current Part** items: **Delete All Zones and
Groups**, and **Resolve Missing Samples** when samples are missing.

## Saving and loading

A whole instrument is a **multi** (`.scm`); a single part is a `.scp` file. The disk button's
**Save and Load** menu has:

- **Save Multi** and **Save Part N**: write back to the file the multi or part was loaded from or
  last saved to, with no dialog. A renamed multi or part is saved as a new file beside it. With
  no file yet, they ask where to save.
- **Save Multi As...** and **Save Part N As...**: refer to samples where they are on disk.
- **as Monolith...**: stores the samples inside the file.
- **with Collected Samples...**: copies the samples to a folder beside the file.
- **Export**: the part as SFZ, which keeps only a small subset of features, and **Collect All
  Samples** or **Collect Part N Samples** into a folder.
- **Load Multi** and **Load Part Into N**
- **Reset Engine To**

**Save Multi** and **Save Part N** are not available for a monolith; use **as Monolith...**.

Loading or saving names the multi or part after its file. Loads can be undone.

Dropping a single `.scm` or `.scp` file on the header asks, then loads it as the multi or into
the selected part.

**Reset Engine To** starts again from **Empty Part**, **16 Empty Parts** or **Basic Synth**, or
from your templates in `Patches/Templates`. A built-in template does not ask for confirmation,
and clears the undo history.

The same menu sets a **Startup Default**: a multi or part that every new instance loads. Choose
**Set Startup Default...** to pick a file, its name to reset to it, or **Clear Startup Default**.
A startup part loads into part 1 of an empty multi. If the file cannot be loaded, the instance
starts empty and the startup default is cleared.
