## Voice limits

Voices are limited at three levels:

- **Engine**: 512 voices in total.
- **Part**: the voice limit button on the part card, from 4 to 512.
- **Group**: the **Polyphony** button in **GROUP SETTINGS**, available in Poly mode. **PART**
  means the group has no limit of its own; otherwise choose 1 to 64 voices.

When a limit is reached, the oldest voice is stolen. Released voices are stolen before held ones.

## Exclusive groups

**Exclusive Group** in **GROUP SETTINGS** puts groups in a choke group. When a group starts a
voice, voices in other groups with the same exclusive group number are quickly faded out. Choose
**New Group** to create a new number.

## Group settings

The rest of the **GROUP SETTINGS** card applies to all selected groups:

- **Volume** and **Pan**
- **Tuning**: -24 to +24 semitones.
- **PB**: pitch bend range down and up, 0 to 48 keys. Zones use this unless they set their own.

The group **ROUTING** pane also has:

- **Vel**: velocity sensitivity for the group.
- **2xOS**: runs the group and its zones oversampled. On by default.
