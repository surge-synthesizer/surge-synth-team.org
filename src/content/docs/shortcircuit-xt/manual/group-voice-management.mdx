## Voice limits

Voices are limited at three levels:

- **Engine**: 512 voices in total.
- **Part**: the voice limit button on the part card, from 4 to 512.
- **Group**: the polyphony button in **GROUP SETTINGS**, available in Poly and Piano mode.
  **PART** means the group has no limit of its own; otherwise choose from 1 to 64 voices.

When a limit is reached, the oldest voice is stolen. Released voices are stolen before held ones.

## Exclusive groups

The exclusive group button in **GROUP SETTINGS** puts groups in a choke group. It reads **OFF**
until a number is chosen. When a group starts a voice, voices in other groups with the same
exclusive group number are quickly faded out. Choose **New Group** to create a new number.

## Group settings

The rest of the **GROUP SETTINGS** card applies to all selected groups:

- **Volume**, up to +12 dB, and **Pan**
- **Tuning**: -24 to +24 semitones.
- **PB**: pitch bend range down and up, 0 to 48 keys. Zones use this unless they set their own.

The group **ROUTING** pane also has:

- **Vel**: velocity sensitivity for the group.
- **HQ**: oversampling for the group and its zones. Click to step through **HQ: OFF**,
  **HQ: ON** and **HQ: AUTO**, or right-click for a menu.
  - **Off (1x)**: never oversamples.
  - **On (2x)**: always runs the group and its zones at twice the sample rate. The default for
    new groups.
  - **Auto (2x when pitched up)**: oversamples only a voice pitched up far enough to alias.
    Imported instruments use this.

## Sounding groups

While a group is producing sound, its row in the group and zone tree shows a speaker. The
speaker stays lit while the group's processors ring out after its voices end.
