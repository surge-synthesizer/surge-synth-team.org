Open the mixer with **MIX** in the header, or Cmd/Ctrl+4.

- **Top**: the four effects of the selected bus.
- **Bottom**: the **OUTPUTS** panel, with a channel strip for each part bus, then the four aux
  buses and **MAIN** at the right. The part strips scroll.
- **Right**: the [browser](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/ui-browser/).

Click a strip to select its bus.

See [Using the Mixer](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/mixer/) for the channel strip controls.
