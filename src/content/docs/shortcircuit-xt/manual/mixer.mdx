Open the mixer with **MIX** in the header, or Cmd/Ctrl+4. For how audio reaches the mixer, see
[Routing](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/structure-routing/).

## Channel strips

The **OUTPUTS** panel at the bottom holds a strip for each bus: **BUS 1** to **BUS 16**,
**AUX 1** to **AUX 4** and **MAIN**. From top to bottom, a strip has:

- **FX**: the bus's four effect slots. The small button turns a slot on or off. Click a slot to
  choose an effect.
- **AUX** (part buses only): four send levels. The glyph beside each send chooses where it is
  taken from: **Pre-FX**, **Post-FX, Pre VCA** or **Post VCA**. The default is Post VCA.
- **Pan**
- **M** and **S** (not on MAIN). While any bus is soloed, the others are silenced.
- **Level** and a meter
- **Output** (not on MAIN): **MAIN** or a plugin output, **OUT 1** to **OUT 20**.

The output menu also has **All Busses to Main** and **Each Bus to Unique Output**. Unique output
sends the part buses to Out 1 to Out 16 and the aux buses to Out 17 to Out 20.

## Bus effects

Click a strip to select its bus. The selected bus's four effects are shown across the top of the
screen, running in series from left to right. The effects are the same as part FX; see
[FX](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/parts-fx/).
