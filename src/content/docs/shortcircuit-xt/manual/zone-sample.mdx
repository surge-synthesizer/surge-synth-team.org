A zone plays one sample, a choice of samples called variants, or no sample at all.

## The sample

The **SAMPLE** tab of the mapping pane edits the lead zone's sample. Double-click the lead zone
on the map to open it, and double-click the waveform to go back to **MAPPING**.

Each sample has:

- **Start** and **End**
- A loop mode: **Loop**, **Loop Alternate**, **Loop Until Release**, **Loop Alternate Until
  Release** or **Loop For Count**
- **∞** to turn looping on, loop **Start** and **End**, **XF** crossfade length, and the crossfade
  curve
- Reverse, which also draws the waveform reversed
- **SRC**: interpolation, **Sinc**, **Linear**, **Zero-Order Hold (Anti-Aliased)** or **Zero Order
  Hold**
- Volume, pan and tuning

The loop controls are hidden until looping is on. While the **AMP EG** is **Sample Gated**, only
**Loop For Count** is available.

The info button beside **File** shows the sample rate, channels, bit depth and length. Click the
sample name to load a different file into the current variant. The arrows beside it step to the
previous or next sample in the same folder.

## Editing on the waveform

Drag the handles on the waveform to set start and end, at the bottom, and loop points, at the
top. Edits reach notes that are already playing.

- Markers snap to slices from WAV cue markers when dragged close to one.
- Hold **Shift** while dragging a marker to snap it to a zero crossing near the mouse.
- Right-click the waveform for the **Zero Crossings** items, which move one marker, or all of them
  with **Snap All to Zero Crossings**, to the nearest zero crossing. A stereo sample crosses where
  the sum of its channels does.

## The loop crossfade

**XF** fades the end of the loop into the material before the loop start. With looping on, each
loop marker has a fade node on the waveform. Drag a node, or Alt-drag a loop marker, to set the
crossfade length by moving sideways and its curve by moving up and down.

The curve runs from 0% (linear) through 100% (equal power, the default) to 150%. It shapes
forward loops only, and is greyed out for the alternate modes.

The crossfade always fits inside the loop and after the sample start. Start, loop start and loop
end stop where they would cut into it.

## Editing several zones

The sample controls act on the same variant of every selected zone. Start, end, loop points and
**XF** are positions in one sample, so dragging or typing them changes only the lead zone. Type
**+N** or **-N** instead to move that marker by N samples in every selected zone.

The pane's menu button, with the **SAMPLE** tab showing, applies these to the same variant of
every selected zone:

- **Restore Loops from Samples**: uses the loop stored in each sample file and turns looping on.
  A sample with no stored loop is left as it is.
- **Copy Current Loop Settings**, **Copy Current Sample Start** and **Copy Current Sample End**:
  copy from the variant shown, fitted to each sample's length.
- **Adjust Loop Starts -1** and **+1**, **Adjust Loop Ends -1** and **+1**: nudge looping variants
  by one sample.

Each is a single undo step.

## The variants

A zone holds up to 16 variants, shown as numbered tabs. Drop a sample on **+** to add one, or on a
tab or the waveform to replace it. To make a zone from several files at once, hold Alt while
dropping them on the map.

The **Variants** menu and **EDIT ALL** appear once a zone has two or more variants.

**Variants** chooses which variant plays for each note:

- **Round Robin**: each in turn.
- **Shuffle**: each once, in random order, before repeating.
- **Random**
- **Random (no repeats)**: never the same variant twice in a row.
- **Unison**: all of them at once.

Each variant has its own copy of the sample controls above. **EDIT ALL** applies edits to every
variant, except start, end, loop points, crossfade and normalization, which stay per variant. With
**EDIT ALL** on, the menu actions above reach every variant.

Right-click a variant tab to copy, paste, delete or normalize it. Paste puts the copy after the
variant clicked, or at the end from **+**, and can go into another zone. With the variant display
focused, Cmd/Ctrl+C copies the variant shown, Cmd/Ctrl+X cuts it and Cmd/Ctrl+V pastes after it.

## Empty zones

An empty zone has a range but no sample. Create one by:

- right-clicking a group in the tree and choosing **Create Empty Zone**, which maps keys 48 to 72
- right-clicking empty space on the map and choosing **Create Empty Zone**, which fills the gap
  around the click
- Cmd/Ctrl-dragging on empty space on the map

An empty zone still plays a voice for each note, running its envelopes, modulators and
processors. With a processor from the **Generators** category, such as Virtual Analog or Sine +,
an empty zone is a synthesizer voice.

The voice lasts as long as the amp envelope. An empty zone with no processors makes no sound.

Drop a sample on an empty zone's **SAMPLE** tab to give it one.
