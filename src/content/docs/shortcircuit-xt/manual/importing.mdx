## Supported files

| Kind | Extensions |
| --- | --- |
| Samples | `.wav`, `.flac`, `.aif`, `.aiff`, `.mp3`, `.opus` |
| Instruments | `.sf2`, `.sfz`, `.exs`, `.gig`, `.akp`, `.multisample` |
| Shortcircuit XT | `.scm` (multi), `.scp` (part) |

Samples must be mono or stereo. WAV and AIFF load from 8 to 32 bit, including 12 and 20 bit, and
WAV also loads 32 and 64 bit float. FLAC loads at any depth from 4 to 32 bit.

## Importing an instrument

Double-click an instrument in the [browser](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/ui-browser/), or drag it onto
the mapping pane or a part card. When dragging, drop on one half to **replace** the part or the
other half to **add to** it.

An import adds new groups to the selected part. It does not create a new part. The whole import
is a single undo step.

Dragging an SF2 file with several instruments asks which one to import. Double-clicking it imports
all of them.

Features Shortcircuit XT cannot import are skipped.

## What each format brings in

- **SF2**: one group per instrument, with key and velocity ranges, root key, tuning and scale
  tuning, sample offsets, loops including loop until release, pan, level, both envelopes with
  key number scaling, the filter, the vibrato and modulation LFOs with their delays, and the
  file's modulators, including preset level and global ones. Zones with an exclusive class get a
  group of their own, in an
  [exclusive group](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/group-voice-management/#exclusive-groups). Reverb
  and chorus sends, fixed key and fixed velocity are not imported.
- **SFZ**: regions with key and velocity ranges, velocity fades, root key (including
  `pitch_keycenter=sample`), keytrack, tuning and transpose, level, pan, offset and end, reverse,
  loops (`loop_mode`, including `one_shot`, and `loop_type`), amp and filter envelopes, filter,
  and common CC and velocity modulation. `<global>`, `<master>` and `<group>` opcodes are
  inherited, and `#include` and `#define` are supported. Some opcodes become group settings:
  - `trigger=release` regions go to a group of their own which plays on release.
  - `sw_last` and `sw_down` become **KeySwitch** and **KeySwitch (Momentary)** trigger
    conditions, and `sw_default` sets the part's default articulation.
  - `group` and `off_by` become exclusive groups. One way chokes import as mutual.
  - `seq_position` on a region makes variants of one zone. On a header it gives each group a
    **Round Robin** > **Cycle** trigger condition.
  - Regions sharing a key and velocity range and split by `lorand` and `hirand` become one zone
    with **Random** variants. `lorand` and `hirand` on a `<group>` become a **Dice per Key Press**
    trigger condition.
- **EXS**: groups with volume, pan, polyphony and exclusive groups; zones with ranges, tuning,
  start and end, reverse, one-shot, release trigger and loops; the filter and both envelopes.
  Round robin groups are imported as parallel zones.
- **GIG**: one group per instrument, with one zone for each velocity and layer split. Other
  dimensions, such as round robin and keyswitch, keep only their first zone. LFOs are not
  imported.
- **AKP**: key and velocity ranges, tuning, pan, level, two envelopes and a low pass filter. Samples
  are looked for beside the `.akp` file; missing samples are skipped.
- **.multisample**: groups, key and velocity ranges and fades, root key, keytrack, tuning, gain,
  sample start and end, reverse and loops, including ping-pong loops and loop fades. Round robin
  samples sharing a mapping become variants of one zone.

## Metadata in sample files

Shortcircuit XT reads the root key, key and velocity ranges and loop from WAV (`smpl` and `inst`
chunks), AIFF and FLAC files. WAV cue and `strc` markers become slices shown on the waveform.

## Missing samples

If a multi or part refers to samples that cannot be found, the **Missing Sample Resolution** screen
lists them. Click **Resolve** to locate a file. With **Auto-Resolve Siblings** on, other missing
files from the same folder are found too.

To reopen the screen, right-click a zone and choose **Resolve Missing Samples**.
