The browser is on the right of every screen.

## Locations

The top of the browser lists locations: your system's drives or home folders, **User Patches**,
and any favourites you add. **ALL**, **OS** and **FAVS** filter the list.

Right-click a location to **Add Favorite...**, remove a favourite, or reveal it on disk. Right-click
a folder in the file list to **Add to Favorites**.

## Files

The file list shows folders and files Shortcircuit XT can load. Double-click a folder to open it,
or **..** to go up.

Instrument files such as SF2, SFZ and GIG have an arrow. Click it to list the instruments and
samples inside. Multi (`.scm`) and part (`.scp`) files open the same way:

- A multi lists **Full Multi**, each active part, and any samples stored in a monolith.
- A part file lists the part and any samples stored in it.

Loading:

- **Double-click** a sample to add it to the selected group, or an instrument to import it into
  the selected part. A multi loads as the whole multi, and a part file or a part of a multi loads
  into the selected part.
- **Drag** files to the mapping pane, the group and zone tree, or a part card. See
  [The Range](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/zone-range/) and
  [Importing Formats](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/importing/). A part dragged onto a part card loads
  into that part.

A sample listed inside any instrument, multi or part file loads as a sample, not as its file.

## The keyboard

Click a file to give the list the keyboard.

- **Up** and **Down** move through the list.
- **Right** enters a folder, or opens an instrument file.
- **Left** closes an instrument file, or goes up a folder and lands on the folder you left.
- **Return** does what a double-click does. On a folder it enters and selects the first entry.
- Typing a name jumps to the first entry starting with it. Letters typed within a second build
  up the name; typing the same letter again moves to the next match.

See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Previewing and auto-load

The footer holds:

- **Auto Preview**, the speaker: plays a sample when you click it or move onto it with the keys.
  Moving onto a folder stops it. On by default.
- **Launch Preview**: plays the last sample clicked or moved onto.
- The slider sets the preview volume.
- **AUTO-LOAD**: clicking or moving onto a sample loads it into the lead zone in place of its
  sample. Off by default.

Auto-load keeps the zone's key and velocity ranges, crossfades, root key, and the variant's
tuning and level. Start, end and loop come from the new sample; a sample with no loop leaves the
zone unlooped. A zone with several variants is left with one. Each load is its own undo step.
Samples inside instrument files are not auto-loaded.
