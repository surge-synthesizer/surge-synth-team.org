The browser is on the right of every screen.

## Locations

The top of the browser lists locations: your system's drives or home folders, **User Patches**,
and any favourites you add. **ALL**, **OS** and **FAVS** filter the list.

Right-click a location to **Add Favorite...**, remove a favourite, or reveal it on disk. Right-click
a folder in the file list to **Add to Favorites**.

## Files

The file list shows folders and files Shortcircuit XT can load. Double-click a folder to open it,
or **..** to go up.

Instrument files such as SF2, SFZ and GIG have an arrow. Click it to list the instruments and
samples inside.

- **Double-click** a sample to add it to the selected group, or an instrument to import it into
  the selected part.
- **Drag** files to the mapping pane, the group and zone tree, or a part card. See
  [The Range](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/zone-range/) and
  [Importing Formats](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/importing/).

Click a file to give the list the keyboard. **Up** and **Down** then move through the list,
**Left** and **Right** open and close an instrument file, and **Return** does what a double-click
does. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Previewing

- **Auto Preview**: plays a sample when you click it. On by default.
- **Launch Preview**: plays the last clicked sample.
- The slider sets the preview volume.
