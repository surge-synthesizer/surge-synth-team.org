Each part has four FX slots, shown when the **PARTS** tab of the edit screen is selected. They run
in series, after the part's level and pan and before the part's bus.

Part FX are separate from the effects on the part's mixer bus. A part playing through its default
bus runs its four part FX, then the bus's four effects.

## Available effects

- Reverb 1
- Reverb 2
- Delay
- Floaty Delay
- Flanger
- Phaser
- TreeMonster
- Nimbus
- Bonsai

The same effects are available on mixer buses.

## Slot controls

- Click the slot's title to choose an effect.
- The power button turns the effect on or off.
- The metronome button, on effects with timed parameters, syncs them to tempo.
- The menu saves and loads effect presets.

Drag a slot onto another to swap them. Hold Shift to move, or Cmd/Ctrl to copy.
