## Enabling MPE

How Shortcircuit XT uses MIDI channels is a single setting for the whole instance. Click the
channel mode button in the header, which reads **OMNI**, **MPE** or **Ch/Oct**, and choose
**MPE**.

In MPE mode every part responds to every channel, and the MIDI channel button on each part is
disabled. MIDI channel 1 is the global channel.

The channel mode is saved with your DAW session, not in multi files. Choose **Use MPE as default
for new instances** from the same menu to make it the default.

## MPE settings

The **MPE Settings** submenu of the channel mode menu applies to all parts:

- **Bend range**: 12, 24, 48 or 96 semitones. The default is 24.
- **MPE Pitch Smoothing**: Off, 10ms, 25ms, 50ms or 100ms.

## What MPE controls

- **Pitch bend** on a member channel bends that note only.
- **Pitch bend** on the global channel reaches the normal **Pitch Bend** source for all parts.
- **Channel pressure** on a member channel becomes the per-note **Pressure** source.
- **CC74** on a member channel becomes the per-note **Timbre** source.
- **Channel pressure** on the global channel becomes **Channel AT**.

**Voice Pitch Bend**, **Pressure** and **Timbre** appear in the **MPE** category of the zone
modulation matrix. Per-note pitch bend always changes pitch; pressure and timbre do nothing until
you route them.

## CLAP note expressions

In CLAP hosts, note expressions are available in the **Note Expressions** category of the zone
modulation matrix: Volume, Pan, Tuning, Vibrato, Expression, Brightness and Pressure.

Volume, Pan and Tuning act on the note directly. The others only act through the matrix.

Polyphonic aftertouch is available as the **Poly AT** source.
