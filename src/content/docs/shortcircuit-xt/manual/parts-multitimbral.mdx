A multi has 16 parts, each an independent instrument. A new multi has only Part 1 active.

Parts are managed in the **PARTS** tab of the edit screen sidebar. Each active part has a card.
**Add Part** activates the next inactive part.

## Choosing which part plays

The channel mode button in the header, **OMNI**, **MPE** or **Ch/Oct**, sets how all parts use
MIDI channels.

- **Omni**: each part's MIDI button sets the channel it listens to, either **Omni** for all
  channels or **Channel 1** to **Channel 16**. Part N starts on channel N.
- **Channel/Octave**: every part plays on every channel, and the channel shifts the note. Channels
  1 to 8 shift up 0 to 7 octaves; channels 9 to 16 shift down 8 to 1 octaves. With a microtuning
  loaded, an octave is the scale's repeat interval.
- **MPE**: every part plays on every channel. See [MPE](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/mpe/).

The **PARTS** panel menu has **Set All to Omni** and **Set to Incremental MIDI Channels**, and
chooses between **Expanded Display**, where each card shows the part's description, and
**Compressed Display**, where the description shows when you hover over the card.

## The part card

- **Name**: click to rename.
- **M** and **S**: mute and solo. A muted part does not respond to new notes.
- **MIDI**: the part's channel, in Omni mode.
- **Level** and **Pan**
- **OUT**: where the part's output goes. See [Routing](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/structure-routing/).
- **Voice limit**: the most voices the part can play, from 4 to 512.
- **Tuning**: in semitones, -36 to +36.
- **Transpose**: shifts incoming notes by -36 to +36 keys before zones are chosen.

Right-click the card or its name for the part menu. The **PARTS** panel menu has the same items
for the selected part.

- **Load Part...** and **Reload Part**, which goes back to the file on disk.
- **Save Part**, **Save Part As...**, **Save Part as Monolith...** and **Save Part with Collected
  Samples...**
- **Initialize Part**: empties the part, keeping its MIDI channel and output.
- **Delete Part**: deactivates the part.
- **Duplicate Part**: copies the part into the first inactive part.
- **Copy Part** and **Paste Part**

Paste replaces the part with the copy, including its name, description, groups, macros and part
FX. The part keeps its own MIDI channel and output. Click a card and use Cmd/Ctrl+C and
Cmd/Ctrl+V to do the same from the keyboard.

## Part settings

With the **PARTS** tab selected, the **SETTINGS** tab beside **MACROS** holds **Default
Articulation**: the latching keyswitch the part arms when no other is live. **Lowest Group** is
the default. See [Trigger Conditions](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/group-triggers/#keyswitches).

## Saving and loading parts

A part saves to a `.scp` file. **Save Part** writes back to the file the part came from, with no
dialog. **Save Part As...** refers to samples where they are, **Save Part with Collected
Samples...** copies them to a folder beside the file, and **Save Part as Monolith...** stores them
inside the file.

Dropping a `.scp` file on a part card replaces that part.
