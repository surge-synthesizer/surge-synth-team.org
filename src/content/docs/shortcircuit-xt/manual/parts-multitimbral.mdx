A multi has 16 parts, each an independent instrument. A new multi has only Part 1 active.

Parts are managed in the **PARTS** tab of the edit screen sidebar. Each active part has a card.
**Add Part** activates the next inactive part.

## Choosing which part plays

The channel mode button in the header, **OMNI**, **MPE** or **Ch/Oct**, sets how all parts use
MIDI channels.

- **Omni**: each part's MIDI button sets the channel it listens to, either **Omni** for all
  channels or **Channel 1** to **Channel 16**. Part N starts on channel N.
- **Channel/Octave**: every part plays on every channel, and the channel shifts the note. Channels
  1 to 8 shift up 0 to 7 octaves; channels 9 to 16 shift down 8 to 1 octaves. With a microtuning
  loaded, an octave is the scale's repeat interval.
- **MPE**: every part plays on every channel. See [MPE](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/mpe/).

The **Part Options** menu at the top of the sidebar has **Set All to Omni** and **Set to
Incremental MIDI Channels**.

## The part card

- **Name**: click for the **Part I/O** menu, with rename, save, load and **Deactivate Part**.
- **M** and **S**: mute and solo. A muted part does not respond to new notes.
- **MIDI**: the part's channel, in Omni mode.
- **Level** and **Pan**
- **OUT**: where the part's output goes. See [Routing](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/structure-routing/).
- **Voice limit**: the most voices the part can play, from 4 to 512.
- **Tuning**: in semitones, -36 to +36.
- **Transpose**: shifts incoming notes by -36 to +36 keys before zones are chosen.

## Saving and loading parts

A part saves to a `.scp` file. **Save Part** refers to samples where they are, **Save Part with
Collected Samples** copies them to a folder beside the file, and **Save Part as Monolith** stores
them inside the file.

Dropping a `.scp` file on a part card replaces that part.
