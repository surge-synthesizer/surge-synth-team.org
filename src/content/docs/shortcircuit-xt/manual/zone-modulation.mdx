Each zone has its own modulators and an 18 row modulation matrix. Every voice runs its own copy.
Groups have a similar set, running once for the group.

| | Zone | Group |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Envelopes | AMP EG plus EG2 to EG5 | GRP EG1 plus GEG2 to GEG5 |
| LFOs | LFO 1 to 4 | GLFO 1 to 4 |
| Phasors | A to D | A to D |
| Randoms | A to D | A to D |
| Envelope followers | 2 | 2 |
| Matrix rows | 18 | 18 |

## Envelopes

The **AMP EG** controls the zone's volume. The other envelopes do nothing until routed in the
matrix. EG2 to EG5 share one panel as tabs; in a group, so do GEG2 to GEG5.

Each envelope has delay, attack, hold, decay, sustain and release, with shape controls for attack,
decay and release. The metronome button in the panel header syncs all of the envelope's times to
the host tempo, so they are set in note values. The menu sets how the envelope follows the note:

- **Gated (DAHDSR)**: holds at sustain until the key is released.
- **No Sustain**
- **Oneshot**
- **Sample Gated** (zones only)

In **No Sustain** and **Oneshot**, the sustain slider becomes a breakpoint, **B**. The AMP EG
title shows the mode, such as **AMP EG (OneShot)**.

Group envelopes also have **Gate on Voice Sounding**, which keeps the envelope gated while any
voice in the group sounds.

## LFOs

Choose a shape from the LFO menu:

- **Sine**, **Triangle**, **Saw**, **Pulse**, **Smooth Noise**, **S&H Noise**, with rate, phase,
  deform and angle, plus delay, attack and release.
- **Step Sequencer**: 1 to 32 steps. Drag to draw.
- **Envelope**: a DAHDSR envelope with a rate multiplier and loop.

The trigger mode sets when the LFO restarts: **KEYTRIG**, **SONGPOS**, **RANDOM**, **RELEASE** or
**ONESHOT**.

The metronome button in the panel header syncs the LFO's rate, and its delay, attack, release and
envelope times, to the host tempo.

## MISC and AUDIO

The **MISC** tab holds the phasors and randoms.

- **Phasors** ramp in time with the song or the voice, at a note division or a ratio of the tempo.
- **Randoms** pick a new value when the voice or group starts, from a choice of distributions.

The **AUDIO** tab holds two envelope followers, taken before or after the processors.

## The modulation matrix

Each row connects a **SOURCE** to a **TARGET** with a depth. A **VIA** source scales the row, and a
curve can reshape the source. The source menus also set smoothing, from 10 to 500 ms.

The curve menu has powers and simple maps such as **x^2** and **1 - x**, and the **Rectifiers**,
**Comparators**, **Waveforms**, **Scale** and **Curve 01 remappers** categories. The remappers are
rising and falling curves for a 0 to 1 source: early or late, slow or fast.

Zone sources include the LFOs and envelopes, the group's LFOs and envelopes (GEG 1 to GEG 5),
MIDI (mod wheel, velocity, release velocity, aftertouch, pitch bend), MIDI CCs, key tracking,
MPE, note expressions, voice state, phasors, randoms, envelope followers and the part's macros.
The **Voice** category includes **Release Countdown**, for
[release triggers](/shortcircuit-xt/manual/group-triggers/#release-triggers).

Zone targets include envelope and LFO parameters, zone tune, pan and amplitude, sample **Start
Pos**, every processor parameter, and the depth of other matrix rows. A negative **Start Pos**
amount can start a voice before the start marker, or past the end marker on a reversed zone, as
far as the sample runs.

The depth tooltip shows the target's value at rest and at full throw. For a bipolar source it
shows both directions, and the depth bar also shows the opposite throw faintly.

If a row targets a processor parameter that is no longer there, because the slot was changed, the
row is greyed and named like **P1.4: none**. Changing the slot back brings the route back.

Right-click a row for **Copy**, **Paste**, **Duplicate to**, **Swap with** and **Move to**. With
one of a row's controls focused, Cmd/Ctrl+C copies the row, Cmd/Ctrl+V pastes over it and
Cmd/Ctrl+X copies it and clears it.

## Macros

Each part has 16 macros, edited in the **MACROS** tab of the mapping pane. A macro can be
**Unipolar**, **Bipolar** or a **Toggle**, and can be renamed. Macros are available as modulation
sources in every zone and group of the part, and appear to your DAW as plugin parameters.
