We love to hear from our users! Our projects have been able to attain the quality they have today
thanks to a dedicated group of musicians who gave patient and clear feedback on bugs and brainstormed
ideas for new and improved features. We invite you to share your feedback too!

The best way to reach the team is either to open an issue on GitHub or
[join our Discord server](https://discord.gg/aFQDdMV) and chat with us. Here are the GitHub issue pages for our projects:

<IssueTrackers />

And don't forget - good bug reports make good software, so feel free to open a GitHub issue! In doing so, there are some guidelines
which may help along the way.

## Guidelines

Good GitHub issues should have clear titles (for example: "Mouse doesn't map properly to filter section when modulating",
not "Bug"). They should contain some key information:

- Full version information for your version of the project you are using. This includes the OS,
  host style, and version. For example: "Windows 64-bit VST3, Surge XT 1.3.4"
- Full information about your operating system. For example: "macOS 10.13.4" or "Ubuntu 18.04 LTS"
- Include the information about your host. This is especially important since there are lot of hosts and we may not
  have had the chance to test your specific one.

Once all of that is included, please include a clear description of the bug - how you demonstrate it and what
you expect. Screenshots help, but clear text is good also.

Here's a great bug report:

_"Surge Windows 10 VST3 version 1.6.1.1 in FL Studio 20. When I am dragging a slider in modulation mode, if I drag
off the end of the slider and keep dragging, the modulation slider can reset to max value. Unmodulated, the slider
doesn't do that"_

That's a real bug report we got. It was a one line fix once we had that clear description!

For features, feel free to be creative. We love to hear ideas!

We strive to fix bugs in a timely fashion. But as always, the best bug is one which comes with a pull request to
fix it. Dive in! The code's not that scary. :)
