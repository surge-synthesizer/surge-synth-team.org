## Caveats

Please take a moment to understand the nightlies. We build Surge XT automatically for all our
platforms on every commit to the
[main branch](https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/surge), which means the code you are about to
download may be minutes old. Depending on the pace of development, it could contain bugs and new
features that do not work, and it may even make patches that future Surge XT versions load
differently, incorrectly, or not at all.

Our minimum advice if you use the nightlies is to **put a limiter on the output**. We have made DSP
errors in nightlies before, which created nasty clicks and pops, and in the worst cases sudden
blasts of noise.

Despite that warning, we work really hard to make the nightlies good. Most of the Surge Synth Team
runs nightlies in their own music-making environment, and the software is generally stable and
robust. Using the nightly, finding a bug and letting us know is how bugs get fixed - see
[contact](/contact) for how to reach us.
