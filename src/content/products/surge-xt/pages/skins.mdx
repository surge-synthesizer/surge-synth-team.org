Many people in the Surge XT community make skins beyond the few we ship with. This page collects a
set of curated skins from community designers for you to download.

Installing a skin is simple: drag the downloaded ZIP file onto a running instance of Surge XT and
you are done.

Although we try to make sure these skins work, and that they contain no inappropriate content,
please use them at your own risk, and understand that support for problems in them may be less
readily available than for the factory skins.

If you want to build one of your own, the [skin development guide](/surge-xt/skins/authoring)
documents the skin engine.
