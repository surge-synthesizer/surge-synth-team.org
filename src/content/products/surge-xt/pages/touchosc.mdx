Since Surge XT 1.3.0 we support the Open Sound Control protocol, which enables remote control of
various aspects of Surge XT over a network connection. One popular tool for remote control is
[TouchOSC](https://hexler.net/touchosc).

Feel free to check out this TouchOSC template on your tablet, or any other device capable of running
TouchOSC. The latest version requires Surge XT 1.3.2.

<Button class="not-prose my-6" href="/Surge_XT_template_1.3.2.tosc">
    Download the template
</Button>

A previous version of the template, requiring Surge XT 1.3.1, is available
[here](/Surge_XT_template_1.3.1.tosc).
