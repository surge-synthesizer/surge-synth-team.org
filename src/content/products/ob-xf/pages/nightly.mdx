## Caveats

Please take a moment to understand the nightlies. We build OB-Xf automatically for all our platforms
on every commit to the [main branch](https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/OB-Xf), which means the
code you are about to download may be minutes old. Depending on the pace of development, it could
contain bugs and new features that do not work, and it may even make patches that future OB-Xf
versions load differently, incorrectly, or not at all.

Our minimum advice if you use the nightlies is to **put a limiter on the output**. We have made DSP
errors in nightlies before, which created nasty clicks and pops, and in the worst cases sudden
blasts of noise.

Despite that warning, we work really hard to make the nightlies good. Testing them is how the final
release gets good, and we need and welcome your feedback - either on
[GitHub](https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/OB-Xf/issues) or on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/spGANHw); see [contact](/contact) for how to reach us.
