<FaqItem q="Is Shortcircuit XT free?">

Yes. It is free and open source. The source is MIT licensed, and released binaries are distributed
under the AGPL v3 or later.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Is it finished?">

No. Shortcircuit XT is in beta, and the download on the [Shortcircuit XT page](/shortcircuit-xt) is
a nightly build of the current code. Use a limiter while experimenting.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Which systems and plugin formats are supported?">

Windows, macOS 10.15 or later, and Linux. Shortcircuit XT runs as a CLAP or VST3 plugin, as an Audio
Unit on macOS, and as a standalone application.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Can it load my sample libraries?">

It imports SF2, SFZ, EXS, GIG, Akai AKP and .multisample instruments, and loads WAV, FLAC, AIFF, MP3
and Opus samples. Features a format has that Shortcircuit XT cannot represent are skipped.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Where are my patches kept?">

In `Documents/Surge Synth Team/Shortcircuit XT`, which also holds themes and presets. A whole
instrument saves as a `.scm` multi and a single part as a `.scp`.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Where do I get help or report a bug?">

Ask in the `#using-shortcircuit` channel of the
[Surge Synth Team Discord](https://discord.com/invite/spGANHw), and report bugs on the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/shortcircuit-xt/issues).

</FaqItem>
