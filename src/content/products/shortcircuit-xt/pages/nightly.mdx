## Caveats

Please take a moment to understand the nightlies. We build Shortcircuit XT automatically for all our
platforms on every commit to the
[main branch](https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/shortcircuit-xt), which means the code you are
about to download may be minutes old. Depending on the pace of development, it could contain bugs
and new features that do not work.

Shortcircuit XT is also still pre-release, so the format it saves in is not yet frozen: a multi you
build today may load differently, incorrectly, or not at all in a later build. Keep your samples,
and do not count on a session surviving an update.

Our minimum advice if you use the nightlies is to **put a limiter on the output**. We have made DSP
errors in nightlies before, which created nasty clicks and pops, and in the worst cases sudden
blasts of noise.

Despite that warning, we work really hard to make the nightlies good. Most of the Surge Synth Team
runs nightlies in their own music-making environment. Using the nightly, finding a bug and letting
us know is how bugs get fixed - either on
[GitHub](https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/shortcircuit-xt/issues) or in the `#sc-development`
channel of our [Discord](https://discord.gg/spGANHw); see [contact](/contact) for more ways to reach
us.
