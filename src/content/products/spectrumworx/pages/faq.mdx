<FaqItem q="Is SpectrumWorx free?">

Yes. It is free and open source. The source is GPL-3.0-or-later, and released binaries link JUCE 8
under its AGPLv3 arm, so they are distributed under the AGPL v3 or later.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="What does a spectral effect actually do?">

It decomposes the incoming audio into the frequency domain, applies its effects to that spectrum
rather than to the waveform, and resynthesizes the result. Effects stack and chain, so a module
works on what the one above it produced.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Is it finished?">

Not yet. There has been no stable release since SpectrumWorx was open sourced, and the download on
the [SpectrumWorx page](/spectrumworx) is a nightly build of the current code. Use a limiter while
experimenting - see the [nightly builds page](/spectrumworx/nightly) for what that means.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Which systems and plugin formats are supported?">

Windows, macOS 10.15 or later, and Linux, all 64-bit. SpectrumWorx runs as a CLAP or VST3 plugin, as
an Audio Unit on macOS, and as a standalone application.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Where did it come from?">

SpectrumWorx was a commercial plugin from Little Endian, who open sourced it in 2024. It is now
maintained by the Surge Synth Team on
[GitHub](https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/SpectrumWorx).

</FaqItem>
