## Caveats

Please take a moment to understand the nightlies. We build SpectrumWorx automatically for all our
platforms on every commit to the
[main branch](https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/SpectrumWorx), which means the code you are about
to download may be minutes old. Depending on the pace of development, it could contain bugs and new
features that do not work, and it may even make presets that future builds load differently,
incorrectly, or not at all.

Our minimum advice if you use the nightlies is to **put a limiter on the output**. A spectral effect
resynthesizes everything it is given, so a DSP error here is as likely to arrive as a sudden blast of
noise as it is to sound merely wrong.

Despite that warning, we work really hard to make the nightlies good. Using the nightly, finding a
bug and letting us know is how bugs get fixed - either on
[GitHub](https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/SpectrumWorx/issues) or on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/spGANHw); see [contact](/contact) for how to reach us.
